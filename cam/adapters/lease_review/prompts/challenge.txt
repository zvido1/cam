IMPORTANT — DEFINITION CASCADE DETECTION:
When a provision's text is identical to the template, but the provision has been flagged due to a definition change (RULE-LS-002), you MUST evaluate the IMPACT of that definition change on this specific provision.

A provision whose text is unchanged but whose meaning has materially changed due to a redefined term is a SUBSTANTIVE_DEVIATION, not COSMETIC_ONLY.

Example: If "Affiliate" is redefined from "50% ownership" to "10% ownership," and a provision allows subletting to "any Affiliate," the subletting right has been dramatically expanded even though the provision text is identical.

When evaluating definition cascades, consider:
1. What does the changed definition expand or narrow?
2. Does this provision reference the changed term?
3. Does the changed meaning materially alter rights, obligations, or risk?
