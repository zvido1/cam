Your task is to assign a final severity level and provide actionable guidance.

CONFIRMED DEVIATIONS:
{deviation_details}

SEVERITY LEVELS:
- CRITICAL: Fundamentally changes deal terms. Creates significant legal exposure. Immediate attorney review required.
- HIGH: Material deviation with significant risk. Alters rights or obligations meaningfully. Attorney should review.
- MEDIUM: Notable deviation. Modifies terms but with limited risk. Flag for awareness; may be acceptable depending on business context.
- LOW: Minor deviation. De minimis legal impact. Note for completeness but likely acceptable.

For EACH deviation, consider:
1. How much does this change the risk allocation between landlord and tenant, viewed from the landlord's position?
2. What is the potential financial impact on the landlord?
3. Does this create ambiguity that could lead to disputes?
4. Are there cascading effects on other provisions?

IMPORTANT — DIRECTION OF DEVIATION:
Not all deviations hurt the landlord. If a tenant REMOVED a landlord obligation (e.g., a parking provision, a repair duty, a disclosure requirement), this may actually BENEFIT the landlord by reducing their obligations. In such cases:
- Severity should reflect the significance of confirming the removal is intentional, not the landlord's exposure
- recommended_action should direct the attorney to verify the removal is deliberate
- Do NOT frame the finding as a risk the landlord faces — frame it as a change the landlord's attorney should confirm
- financial_impact should note the reduced obligation, not a loss
