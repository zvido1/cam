        Why CAM exists
        Most AI systems are built to produce an answer — even when the evidence is weak, ambiguous, or contested. In document review, that creates a real risk: hidden uncertainty gets packaged as confidence. CAM™ was built to ask a harder question first: has this conclusion actually earned the right to be asserted?
        ⚖️
        What CAM does differently
        CAM runs multiple independent AI evaluators on the same clause comparison, preserves disagreement instead of averaging it away, and then uses a governance layer to decide whether the system should assert, qualify, challenge, or withhold a conclusion. Abstention is a valid outcome.
        🎯
        Applied here: lease deviation analysis
        This tool applies CAM to one of the most consequential review tasks in commercial real estate: comparing a tenant lease against a reference form and surfacing what materially changed. CAM is not just looking for differences — it is judging whether those differences are important enough, stable enough, and well-supported enough to matter.
        🔍
        What CAM is doing right now
        Each lease moves through a staged pipeline: document parsing and identity checks, rules-based detection of exception language and qualifier shifts, parallel evaluation by independent models, an adversarial challenge round, severity assessment, and output generation. The goal is not speed alone — it is traceable, defensible review.
        📋
        What you'll get in results
        You’ll get a working review system, not just a list of flags: clause-by-clause summaries, document comparison in lease order, notes and draft decisions, an annotated tenant lease, and contract-level tracking of what is still open, resolved, or no longer a deviation.
        🔎
        Why the audit trail matters
        CAM’s differentiator is that it shows its work. For each clause, you can inspect the reference text, tenant text, what changed, what each evaluator concluded, what CAM challenged, and how the final outcome survived stricter review. If a finding seems wrong, you can trace it back to the exact step where it was made.
        🎚️
        What the scores are telling you
        CAM does not treat confidence as a single number. It looks at agreement, evidence support, reasoning strength, and structural fragility. The Assertion Sensitivity Gap (ASG) shows how much a conclusion depends on interpretation: a low gap suggests the finding survives both permissive and strict review, while a high gap signals that a lawyer should examine it more closely.
        💬
        Ask questions while CAM works
        While processing runs, you can use the chat panel to ask about provisions, scoring, review strategy, or what CAM is checking at each stage. The goal is to make the wait productive — and to make the system easier to understand before you open the results.

          How CAM Analyzes Your Lease

          Unlike single-AI tools, CAM uses a multi-stage evaluation pipeline designed for accuracy and transparency:

          1. Three AI models from different providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI) independently analyze each provision without seeing each other's work.
          2. An automated rules engine checks for 8 specific deviation patterns (exception clauses, definition changes, numerical differences, obligation shifts, and more).
          3. A challenger AI probes flagged findings to verify accuracy and catch false positives.
          4. Severity is assessed based on legal and financial impact to prioritize what matters most.

          Each finding shows you exactly what each evaluator concluded, which rules triggered, and how the final verdict was reached. Full transparency, not a black box.

          Click any finding card to expand it and see the full AI analysis, evaluator reasoning, and detection rules.

          What the confidence indicators mean

          After evaluating each clause, CAM assesses how certain the analysis is. These indicators appear in the sidebar and on individual findings:

          • ✓ Verified — All AI evaluators agreed. The finding is reliable.
          • ⚠ Complex — A conclusion was reached, but evaluators had differing interpretations. Worth a careful read.
          • ○ Review — AI confidence was moderate on this clause. Consider checking it manually.
          • ⚠ Uncertain — Evaluators could not reach confident agreement. Manual review is recommended.

          The “Analysis confidence” line at the top of each contract summary shows what percentage of clauses were evaluated with high confidence. Clauses flagged as Uncertain or Review are not necessarily errors — they indicate the AI found the clause more difficult to assess definitively.

          Privacy & Data Handling

          AI Providers: Document text is processed by the following AI providers for analysis:

          • Anthropic (Claude) — API data never used for training. 7-day abuse monitoring retention.
          • OpenAI (GPT) — Opted out of training by default. 30-day retention.
          • Google (Gemini) — Opt-in only for data sharing. 55-day retention.
          • xAI (Grok) — API data siloed from training.

          None of these providers train their models on data submitted through their APIs.

          App-Level Retention:

          • Uploaded documents: Retained for 24 hours to support adding provisions or leases. Immediately deleted on user request or after 24-hour expiry.
          • Analysis results: Retained for 24 hours via your unique link. Immediately deleted on user request or after 24-hour expiry.
          • Anonymized metadata: Provision types analyzed, severity distributions, and processing metrics are retained (with no client content) to improve detection accuracy.
